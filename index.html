Build the evidence pack before options are priced

ASCI guidance is better read as ASIC Moneysmart guidance in plain terms: compare repayments, interest rates, costs and features, not only the headline number. For a Brisbane first buyer, that means preparing the file before lender options are discussed. A clean pack reduces guesswork and makes the broker conversation more concrete.

  1. Gather payslips, tax returns where relevant, bank statements and evidence of existing commitments.
  2. Record deposit source, genuine savings expectations, gift funds if any, and an estimate of purchase costs.
  3. List the property type, target suburbs such as Brisbane CBD, Chermside, Carindale, South Brisbane or Indooroopilly, and whether the dwelling is established, off the plan or construction linked.
  4. State whether grants, schemes or first home buyer concessions need to be checked before a lender is chosen.

Do not treat a locality list as a rate advantage. Brisbane context helps frame practical questions about property documents, strata materials for an apartment, building contracts for construction, and settlement logistics for a first purchase.

Interrogate the shortlist logic in plain English

A useful recommendation should survive a simple challenge: why this lender, why this product, and what was ruled out. Ask the broker to separate policy fit from price. A lender may price sharply yet be less workable if servicing methodology, evidence rules, valuation approach or feature settings do not suit the file.

  • Servicing: what assumptions sit behind borrowing capacity and which debts are treated conservatively.
  • Deposit path: whether lenders mortgage insurance could apply, how it changes total cost, and whether a different structure is worth comparing.
  • Valuation risk: what happens if the purchase price and bank valuation diverge.
  • Features: whether offset, redraw, extra repayments, portability or split loan settings matter for the next few years.

For investors the same discipline applies, but rental income, tax advice and loan structure should be checked before product choice. For refinancing, the current loan must be measured against the proposed loan across fees, repayments and remaining term. For construction, land timing, building contract stages and progress payments need answers before any borrowing figure is relied on.

Compare total cost, flexibility and future change

Rate matters, yet the better decision unit is total cost plus flexibility. A lower rate can be offset by fees, restrictive redraw, weak offset utility or a structure that complicates a later move. Before an application is treated as ready, request a side by side view of the recommended options.

Close the gaps before the application moves

The final step is a readiness check. You should be able to repeat the recommendation without jargon: loan purpose, repayment type, lender rationale, key features, remaining documents and the next milestone. If any answer is vague, the file is not ready to lodge.

  • Purpose line: one sentence stating first purchase, refinance, investment, construction, commercial or debt consolidation.
  • Capacity line: the assumptions used for borrowing capacity and the evidence that could still change the result.
  • Cost line: repayments, fees, features and the effect of lenders mortgage insurance where relevant.
  • Settlement line: contract dates, valuation steps, building stages if applicable and any condition that could delay approval.

Debt consolidation through refinancing needs extra care because total cost, repayment discipline and loan term can move in different directions. A broker should show the trade off plainly rather than present consolidation as automatically cleaner.

  1. Set the file purpose. Write one sentence naming the purpose: first home purchase, refinance, investment, construction, commercial or debt consolidation.
  2. Assemble evidence. Prepare income evidence, deposit details, current loan information if relevant, property type and expected purchase costs.
  3. Request shortlist reasons. Ask for a plain explanation of why each lender fits policy, servicing, deposit, features and settlement needs.
  4. Compare beyond rate. Check repayments, fees, offset, redraw, fixed or variable settings, future refinance options and insurance effects.
  5. Confirm readiness. Before lodging, identify every missing document and any assumption that could change borrowing capacity or approval timing.
